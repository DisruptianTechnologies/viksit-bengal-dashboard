# Product Context — Viksit Bengal Dashboard

## 1. Product Summary
Viksit Bengal Dashboard is an authoritative, public-facing and administrative governance platform tracking West Bengal's economic and infrastructural transformation toward a **₹85 Lakh Crore ($1 Trillion USD) GSDP economy by 2033 (Phase 1)** and a **₹425 Lakh Crore ($5 Trillion USD) centenary economy by 2047 (Phase 2)**.

## 2. Target Personas
1. **Citizens of West Bengal (নাগরিক)**:
   - Track government commitments and localized progress in their home district.
   - Interact with **Sonar Bondhu** bilingual AI assistant for policy queries.
   - Join Sabai Foundation citizen observer membership.
2. **Policy Researchers & Economists (গবেষক)**:
   - Audit official gazettes, government orders, and statistical releases.
   - Submit field evidence for peer review with TIER_1/2/3 categorization.
   - Export granular district economic data in CSV/PDF formats.
3. **State Administrators & Directorate Officials (প্রশাসক)**:
   - Register new cabinet commitments with responsible directorates and deadlines.
   - Transition lifecycle stages (`ANNOUNCED` $\rightarrow$ `UNDER_IMPLEMENTATION` $\rightarrow$ `DELIVERED`).
   - Monitor real-time scraping runs, daily delta tally feeds, and financial outlay reconciliations.

## 3. Macroeconomic Benchmarks (INR / USD)
- **Current Baseline (2024-25)**: ₹21.10 Lakh Crore Realized GSDP (Baseline ₹17.85 Lakh Crore).
- **Phase 1 (2033 Milestone)**: ₹85.00 Lakh Crore GSDP ($1 Trillion USD equivalent) at 13.0% CAGR.
- **Phase 2 (2047 Centenary Vision)**: ₹425.00 Lakh Crore GSDP ($5 Trillion USD equivalent) with ₹28.50 Lakh per capita income.
- **23 Districts**: Complete coverage across 6 economic zones (Greater Kolkata, North Bengal, Rarh Bengal, Coastal, Junglemahal, Central).
